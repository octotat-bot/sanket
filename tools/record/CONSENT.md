# Consent to take part: Sanket ISL recognition test recordings

Paper copy of the consent shown on screen in `/dev/record` (consent version 1). Keep the two in sync:
the on-screen text is `CONSENT_TEXT` in `web/src/app/dev/record/record-client.tsx`.

**Project:** Sanket, real-time Indian Sign Language (ISL) word recognition (a student project by
Mukund Mangla). It recognizes isolated words from a small vocabulary. It is not a translator.

**What happens:** you watch a short reference clip of each word, sign it, and do a few everyday
movements that are not signs, in front of a laptop webcam. The recording tool runs in the browser on
this laptop.

1. **You must be 18 or older to take part.**
2. **Keypoints only.** The tool records keypoints only: numbers describing where your body and hands
   are in each video frame. No video, images or audio are recorded or stored.
3. **One use only.** Your keypoints are used only to measure how accurately the Sanket ISL word
   recognizer works on real webcams, and for nothing else. They are not used to train the model.
4. **Private, never published.** Your keypoint files are kept private by the project and are never
   published or shared. Only overall accuracy numbers, combined across all participants, may appear in
   the project's results.
5. **Withdrawal by code.** No name or contact details are recorded. After you agree, you get a random
   participant code. Write it down: to withdraw later, send the code to Mukund and every recording with
   that code is deleted. The code is **not** written on this form, so this form can't be linked to your
   recordings.
6. **Voluntary.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, and anything not saved is
   discarded.

I confirm that I am 18 or older. I have read the above, my questions have been answered, and I agree to
take part.
