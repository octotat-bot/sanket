# Consent to take part: Sanket ISL recognition test recordings

**Project:** Sanket, real-time Indian Sign Language (ISL) word recognition (a student project by
Mukund Mangla). It recognizes isolated words from a small vocabulary. It is not a translator.

**What happens:** you sign a list of ISL words, and do a few everyday movements that are not signs, in
front of a laptop webcam. The recording tool runs in the browser on this laptop.

**What is recorded:** only *keypoints*: numbers describing where your body and hands are in each
frame. **No images, video or audio are recorded or stored.** No name or contact details are attached
to the recordings. They are identified only by a random participant code shown on screen. That code
is **not** written on this form, so this form can't be linked to your recordings.

**How it is used:** to measure how well the recognizer works on real webcams, and possibly to adapt it.
Results may be published only as aggregate numbers (for example, accuracy). The keypoint files are kept
private by the project and are not published.

**Your choices:** taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. If you
noted your participant code, you can later ask for your recordings to be deleted.

I have read the above, my questions have been answered, and I agree to take part.
